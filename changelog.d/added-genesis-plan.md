- A finished project brief can be compiled into a project plan
  (`ataniq.project-plan/1`, `internal/genesis/plan`): milestones and tasks
  chosen from a versioned template of the brief's kind (software, publishing
  or media), with gates, a first demo, every external service named, a
  monthly ceiling, an estimate band and a never-list. The plan is a proposal
  that binds nothing, the owner's own words are quoted in it as untrusted
  notes and choose nothing, and nothing calls the compiler yet.
